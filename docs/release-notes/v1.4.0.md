## Tickbird 1.4.0

A smarter connection dialog and a new, smaller **Manage Connections** window.

### New
- **Paste a connection string**: paste `postgresql://user:password@host:5432/db`, `crate://...` or `influxdb://...` into the new URL box and
  the form fills itself in (server type, host, port, user, password, database, SSL). The pasted text is cleared so a password does not stay on screen.
- **Notes while you type**: Tickbird points out a likely mistake as you go, such as the HTTP port for CrateDB, a scheme or path typed into the host,
  or a port typed after the host. A new connection gets a suggested name.
- **Test connection, step by step**: the test shows which step failed (address, port, sign-in, database) with a hint of what to try. When it passes,
  you can pick from the databases found on the server.
- **Manage Connections, redesigned**: opens at 720 x 480 instead of 1180 x 420 and can be resized.
  - Search by name, host or group.
  - The list is grouped by database type first and by group second; click a heading to collapse or expand it.
  - Addresses and the green "open" marker are aligned on the right.
  - Keyboard: Up and Down move, Enter connects, F2 edits, Delete removes.
- **Colours**: give a connection or a group a colour (nine to choose from). A connection shows its own colour, otherwise its group's.
